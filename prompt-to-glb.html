Workflow · v2 · one full run

From one theme
to a finished scene.

A rusted 1950s gas station, built end to end with ComfyUI, Pixal3D and Blender, and driven by Claude through MCP. Every image below comes from that single run.

Read the twelve stepsSee the tools

Pick a theme. Choose by number. Get a scene you can render.

Twelve steps, one conversation

You make the choices. Claude runs ComfyUI and Blender between them.

01 · Theme

A rusted 1950s gas station in the desert▌

Claude turns the theme into a list of props before drawing anything: the station, a gas pump, a price sign, a car, a soda machine, oil cans, tires, an air compressor and a bench.

02 · Flux 2 Turbo in ComfyUI

Four concepts per prop

Every prop gets four options. You answer with numbers and can keep two of the same prop. Real brand logos are erased with Qwen Image Edit before anything goes to 3D.

03 · Flux 2 with reference images

See the final shot before any 3D

The chosen props are fed back as references and combined into three possible final images. You know what you are building before a single mesh exists. This run went with the sunset.

04 · Qwen Image Edit 2511 with the Multiple Angles LoRA

Four angles of the same object

The front view is generated first, then the sides and the back are generated from it, so the pump stays the same pump all the way around.

05 · Pixal3D in ComfyUI

Image to 3D

Pixal3D builds a dense textured mesh from the four views, with a real back instead of a guessed one. It is detailed, but far too heavy for a game: about 700,000 triangles for one pump.

06 · Blender and Quad Remesher

Retopology

Quad Remesher rebuilds the pump as clean quads, 28 times lighter. The shape stays, the noise goes. The low poly on the right carries no extra geometry: its detail comes back from the bake.

07 · Blender bake in Cycles

UVs and baked maps

The low poly is unwrapped, then the high poly is baked onto it: base color, an ORM map packing occlusion, roughness and metal, and a normal map that carries the small dents and bolts. Four 2K textures, ready for any engine.

08 · Thirteen GLB files

Every prop, game ready

The same treatment for every prop: thirteen GLB files at 12,000 to 37,000 faces each. When the four views drifted, as with the station wagon, one image gave the better mesh. The building came out sharper with TRELLIS.2.

09 · Blender

A real 3D set

Every prop is a mesh in one Blender scene, scaled to real sizes and placed on a road through a desert dressed with BlendKit plants. The camera can go anywhere: this is a set, not a painting.

10 · Cycles

Light and atmosphere

A sunset HDRI and ground haze set the mood. This is the scene before the wear pass.

11 · Decals from ComfyUI

Wear and story

ComfyUI also paints the wear: a faded sign for Dustline, a made up brand, plus rust streaks, grime, oil stains, cracks, skid marks and blown sand. Each one is a transparent plane laid on the wall or wrapped onto the road.

12 · Final render

One ultrawide frame

Rendered in Cycles at 5120 × 2160, in the 21:9 ultrawide format. A wall lamp left on above the door is the only warm light in the station. The scene itself stays a set of GLB files you can open in Unreal Engine, Unity or any viewer.
