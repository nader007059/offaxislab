Quick tests that are not tools yet. Each one is a short video on the channel.

01 · TouchDesigner and Unreal Engine

An LED wall that follows your hand

A webcam reads the hand with MediaPipe in TouchDesigner. Its position goes to Unreal Engine, where a wall of cubes pushes out and lights up under the hand. Made with interactive LED screens at events in mind.

  • Input: a plain webcam
  • Tracking: MediaPipe hands in TouchDesigner
  • Output: a cube wall in Unreal Engine, live

02 · Unreal Engine and MetaHuman

One video in, a MetaHuman out

A test of the MetaHuman Animator motion capture from a single video. No suit and no markers: the body motion from the clip lands on a MetaHuman in Unreal Engine. The person in the input video is generated with AI.

  • Input: one video, no mocap suit
  • Solve: MetaHuman Animator in Unreal Engine
  • Output: body animation on a MetaHuman
